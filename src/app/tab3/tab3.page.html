About the App

Product Inventory is a simple inventory management application designed to help users keep track of their products, categories, prices, and available stock.

✨

Features

➕
Add Products

Add new products to your inventory.

🔍
Search Products

Quickly find products using the search bar.

🎯
Filter by Category

Organize products based on their category.

✏️
Edit Products

Update product information whenever needed.

🗑️
Delete Products

Remove products from your inventory.

☁️
Cloud Storage

Product data is stored using Firebase Firestore.

📋

Product Information

Each product contains important information that makes it easier to manage and monitor your inventory.

Name Product name
Category Product category
Price Product price
Quantity Available stock
Stock Status Current inventory level
Category Filter Filter products by category
